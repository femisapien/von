## Read

- **judge_heldout 55.0 → 64.3% (p=0.017).** First checkpoint in this program above chance on the pairwise judge
  set. Both Von-1.2 and Jeff sit at 52–57. The judge/diversity rows did it; the "nano judge ceiling" was a corpus

- **jabr_v2 −3.1 vs r1 (p=0.035).** The extra rows cost in-distribution jabr. Universal rows went from 100% of the

## Decision

r2 is the better checkpoint on the balanced read: it clears Von-1.2 everywhere r1 did plus judge, and only gives
back jabr. Neither r1 nor r2 beats Jeff on probes. Step 2 (9B pseudo-labels, measured throughput first) stays the
next lever; the Noul polarity defect needs its own targeted data, not more volume.
