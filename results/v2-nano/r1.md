## Read

- First pooled PASS in the program: +6.0 pp over Von-1.2 (p<0.001) on the same corpus. Every encoder-side experiment (gliclass init, judge-mix, stack) was null; the trunk+head is the lever.
- Gains are broad, not one suite: jabr +7.7, jev_standard +18.1, Noul +8.5, Choice +5.2 (all PASS). Score +4.3 (not resolvable) — the Brier objective kept Score, where Jeff lost 7.3 against Von.
- Judge held-out 55.0% (Von 51.7, Jeff 57.0): the nano judge wall stands. Nothing here touches it.
- jev_hard flat (35.1 vs 37.8, p=0.69): the chains lever on Von is independent of this and still applies.
- vs Jeff-0.8B: ahead on jabr (+6.7 PASS), Noul (+7.7 PASS), Score (+9.3); behind on probes (-26.9 FAIL). Jeff's 94% on Hagetino's probes is its data engine (broad task mix), not its trunk — same trunk here scored 67%.
- Noul yes-bias: evidence_noul 14/14 answered "yes" at conf 0.78–0.93 (expected 10 no / 4 yes); all-suite Noul picks 253 yes vs 195 expected true. Same polarity failure Von-1.2 showed on criteria-bearing Noul (#21). Not tuned: probes are held out.
